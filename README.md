# Doubt-Hub
TEAM NO:27
MEMBERS:ELVIN,ANTONY,BEN

DESCRRIPTION:
              Our Doubt Hub is an online platform designed to make academic help simple, quick, and accessible for every student. Instead of waiting for the next class or office hours, students can post their academic doubts anytime, from any subject, and get direct responses from their teachers.

Teachers, in turn, get a centralized dashboard where they can view all pending doubts, respond at their convenience, and keep track of previously answered questions — making the learning process more organized and efficient for everyone involved.

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
                  .Simple and intuitive design makes it easy for students and teachers to use the platform without technical hassle.
                  .Students can raise doubts related to any subject or topic without needing to wait for class time.
                  .Reduces delays in getting academic help, improving overall learning outcomes.

OBJECTIVE:
          .To provide a centralized platform where students can post their academic doubts anytime, without depending on physical class hours.
          .To enable direct communication between students and teachers for quick and accurate doubt resolution.
          .To maintain a proper record of all doubts asked and answered, allowing students to refer back to previous solutions.

TECHNOLOGY USED:CSS,JAVA,HTML,CLAUDE
